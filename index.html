About Us - Balaji Arts

Balaji Arts is a professional Industrial Signage, UV Printing, Name Plate and Safety Signage Solutions company based in Pune, Maharashtra. We provide customized visual communication and identification solutions for industries, factories, offices, commercial spaces and businesses.

With a strong focus on quality, durability, precision and professional finishing, we manufacture a wide range of products including Industrial Name Plates, Metal Name Plates, Aluminium & SS Name Plates, Safety Signages, Fire & Emergency Signages, Industrial Labels, Vinyl Stickers, ACP Sign Boards, Acrylic Signages and UV Printed Products

Our expertise combines modern printing and signage techniques with practical industrial requirements. From machine identification plates and electrical panel labels to safety boards, directional signage and corporate branding, we offer customized solutions according to the customer's size, material, design and application requirements.
